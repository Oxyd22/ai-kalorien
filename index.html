How the AI learned

The prompt isn’t “just ChatGPT.” It was sharpened against real meals and a Buddy benchmark.

  1. v2 — photo & text POC Camera / library / text, traffic light, correction, Health log. First portion heuristics.
  2. Rethink portions Away from “breakfast = 400–700 kcal.” Estimate grams first, ~26 cm plate as scale, account for photo overestimation.
  3. Benchmark with the Buddy project Compared models and strategies. Insight: summing and portion math shouldn’t live in the LLM — use deterministic steps.
  4. v3 — barcode, label, multi-correct Exact paths for packaged food. Correction loop up to 10×. Notes accumulate. 156 → 271 actions.

What the AI may do — and what not

  • Photo/Text: Apple Intelligence returns structured nutrition.
  • Barcode: Open Food Facts × grams — no LLM.
  • Label: OCR reads the table; AI extracts only “per 100 g”; Shortcuts does portion math.
  • Correction: “Half” = math. “Different food” = vision again with your note.
